Privacy Policy

eBay Listing Writer is a personal tool for creating eBay listings from item photographs.

Information We Process

Uploaded photographs are processed to generate listing descriptions and are not permanently stored by the application.

eBay Account Connection

eBay authorization tokens are stored in an encrypted browser cookie. The application does not store eBay user data in a server-side database.

Third-Party Services

Photographs and listing information are processed by Anthropic to generate listings and are sent to eBay when the user chooses to post.

Data Control

Users may disconnect their eBay account and clear browser cookies to remove the saved connection.

Contact

Privacy questions may be directed to the application owner.
